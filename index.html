A few things I’ve been thinking about lately and why they matter to me.

"llm_optimization_and_evals"
why_it_matters

When I worked on prompt optimization at Amazon, a change that fixed one group of examples could make another group worse. That got me interested in how we design evals that catch regressions and tell us whether a change will hold up on new data. GOT prompt optimization project.

"adaptive_machine_learning"
why_it_matters

At TCS Research, I saw prediction accuracy drop when vehicles moved between regions. I’m interested in how a system can detect that shift and decide whether the current model is still good enough, whether to switch models, or whether to retrain. NWDAF research.

"multimodal_evaluation"
why_it_matters

In our traffic surveillance benchmark, the stronger detector wasn’t always part of the stronger tracking pipeline. I’m interested in measuring how errors carry through detection, tracking, and counting, because a single score can hide where a system breaks. Traffic surveillance research.

"gpu_kernels"
why_it_matters

I’m learning CUDA from the basics. I started with vector addition and am now looking at what happens when the GPU schedules blocks and executes threads in warps. Learning GPU Kernels.

"harness_engineering"
why_it_matters

I’m learning how the tools, feedback, and checks around an AI agent affect its work. I’m especially interested in how a good setup makes it easier to reproduce failures and tell whether a change helped.
